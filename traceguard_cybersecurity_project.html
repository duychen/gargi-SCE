THREAT LANDSCAPE

Three Risks You Should Know

Understanding common login risks helps users and organisations respond safely.

🤖

Credential Stuffing

Stolen password pairs from other breaches may be tried on different sites. Use unique passwords for every account.

Defence: unique passwords + MFA
🔐

Brute-Force Attempts

Repeated password guesses may target an account. Rate limits, monitoring and sensible lockout policies can help.

Defence: rate limiting + alerts
🎣

Phishing

Fake login pages may trick people into sharing credentials. Check the website address before signing in.

Defence: verify URLs + MFA
🦠

Ransomware

Malware can disrupt access to files. Updates and tested backups reduce the impact.

Defence: updates + offline backups
